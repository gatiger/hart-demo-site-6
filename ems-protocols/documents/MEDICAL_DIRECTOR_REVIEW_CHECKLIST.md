# EMS Medical Director Review Checklist

This internal checklist tracks items that must be confirmed before the native EMS protocols are published.

- This file is not loaded by the protocol app or service worker.
- Check an item only after the Medical Director or named responsible party approves it.
- Add reviewer, date, and required correction under the applicable item.
- Update this checklist whenever clinical content, diagrams, tables, outside references, or approval language changes.

## Final release approval

- [ ] Compare every native page word-for-word with the signed official source.
- [ ] Confirm grammar/punctuation corrections, formatting, red warnings, and merged pages did not change clinical meaning.
- [ ] Confirm every medication name, concentration, dose, maximum dose, route, interval, age/weight range, contraindication, and medical-control requirement.
- [ ] Confirm every native table and flowchart value.
- [ ] Confirm adult and pediatric protocols remain separate and similarly named pages open the correct version.
- [ ] Confirm every internal cross-reference opens the intended adult, pediatric, general, or resource page.
- [ ] Confirm effective date, protocol version, Medical Director, EMS Director, and agency name.
- [ ] Identify the controlling signed record, its storage location, and the process for approving and recording future revisions.
- [ ] Give written authorization to publish the native HTML version for operational use.
